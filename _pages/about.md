I am a researcher working on **blockchain systems**. My research interests include Layer-1 and Layer-2 scalability, state growth, storage efficiency, databases, security, and trusted execution environments (TEE).

I received my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from [Seoul National University (SNU)](https://en.snu.ac.kr/) under the supervision of [Prof. Soo-Mook Moon](https://altair.snu.ac.kr/smoon/). I also earned my B.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Seoul National University.
